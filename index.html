Frequently asked questions

What do you provide?

A professionally prepared multilingual translation of your valid driver’s licence, plus destination-specific driving-document guidance. It supplements your licence — it does not replace it, and it is not a government-issued International Driving Permit.

Do I need this for my trip?

It depends on your licence origin and destination. Some trips may also require an official IDP. Check your destination before ordering.

How long is the translation available?

We offer 1-, 2-, and 3-year service options for the private translation document. This is not the same as official IDP permit validity, which depends on the issuing authority and destination rules.

Where can I use this service?

Requirements and acceptance vary by destination. Use our destination checker for guidance on your specific trip.

How quickly can I get my translation?

Digital delivery is typically within about 2 hours after a complete application. Printed copies take longer depending on shipping.

Is this an official IDP?

No. This is an independent private translation service. Depending on your licence origin and destination, an official IDP may still be required in addition to your original driving licence.
