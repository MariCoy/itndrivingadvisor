Frequently asked questions

What do you provide?

A multilingual licence translation plus destination guidance. Use with your licence — not a replacement or government-issued permit.

Do I need this for my trip?

Depends on licence and destination. Some trips need a government-issued permit too. Check before ordering.

How long is the translation available?

1-, 2-, or 3-year service options. Not the same as official permit validity.

Where can I use this service?

Requirements and acceptance vary by destination. Use our destination checker for guidance on your specific trip.

How quickly can I get my translation?

Digital delivery is typically within about 2 hours after a complete application. Printed copies take longer depending on shipping.

Government-issued?

No — private translation only. A government permit may still be required.
